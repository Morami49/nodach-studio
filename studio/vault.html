Sign-ins

Owner only. Link an email so that signing in with it (Google or an emailed link) opens this account, not a separate email account. The address is hashed on the hub and never stored; the shared record keeps only its SHA-256 fingerprint as an account.link event, and unlinking adds another event (nothing is overwritten).
